# Meta Ads Playbook

Community-editable best practices the marketing manager uses as background knowledge.
Edit `src/mcp/resources/playbook.md` and open a pull request to improve it.

## 1. How to read results

- Judge ads on **cost per primary result (CPA)** and **ROAS**, not on CTR alone. CTR is a diagnostic.
- Don't judge an ad before it has spent roughly **2–3× your target CPA** or ~3,000 impressions ("learning").
- Compare an ad with **its own history** (fatigue) and with **the account** (winners/losers).
- Look at trends over 7–14 days; single days are noisy (attribution can still update for ~7 days).

## 2. Creative is the biggest lever

- **Hook (first 3 seconds)** decides everything for video. Aim for a hook rate (3s views / impressions) ≥ 25–30%.
  Strong hooks: a bold claim, a visible result (before/after), a question to the viewer, a pattern break,
  a face talking straight to camera, on-screen text that states the benefit.
- **Hold rate** (ThruPlays / 3s views) ≥ 15% means the body keeps attention. Show the payoff early.
- Formats that usually work for e-commerce: UGC / testimonial video, founder story, demo / how-to,
  before–after, problem → solution, comparison, unboxing, FAQ / objection handling.
- Always design for **sound off** (captions, on-screen text) and **9:16** for Reels/Stories.
- Put the **offer and CTA** on screen, not only in the caption.

## 3. Testing method

- Change **one variable at a time**: hook, angle/message, format, offer, audience, landing page.
- Run 3–5 creatives per ad set; let Meta's delivery pick, then cut the clear losers.
- Give each test enough budget to reach a verdict (≈ 2–3× target CPA per creative).
- When a winner appears, make **iterations**: same angle, new hooks; same hook, new format.
- Keep a log of what was tested and what won (the saved reports do this).

## 4. Creative fatigue

- Signs: frequency rising (≥ 3 for prospecting) while CTR falls ≥ 20% and/or CPA rises ≥ 25% vs the ad's own past.
- Fixes, in order: new hook on the same video → new creative on the same angle → new angle → broaden audience.
- Don't kill a fatigued former winner abruptly if nothing better exists; shift budget gradually to replacements.

## 5. Budget

- Move budget **gradually** (≈ 20% per change) toward ad sets with the best efficiency (share of results > share of spend).
- Cut ads that have spent 1.5–2× target CPA with no result.
- Keep ~10–20% of budget for testing new creatives.
- Retargeting is usually cheapest per result but small; don't let it hide weak prospecting.

## 6. Funnel diagnosis

| Symptom                              | Likely cause                                          | What to try                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Low hook rate                        | Weak opening                                          | New first 3 seconds, face + bold claim, motion in frame 1                   |
| Good CTR, low landing page view rate | Slow/broken page, accidental clicks                   | Speed up page (mobile), fix redirects, check placement                      |
| Good LPV, low add-to-cart            | Offer/price/page doesn't convince or doesn't match ad | Match ad promise on page, show reviews, clearer price/offer, bundles        |
| Many carts, few purchases            | Checkout friction, delivery cost, trust               | COD, free/clear delivery, fewer form fields, trust badges, WhatsApp support |
| CPM up, CTR stable                   | Saturation / auction competition                      | New audiences, new creatives, check seasonal competition                    |

## 7. Offers that move sales (test, don't assume)

- Free delivery (or threshold free delivery), cash on delivery, bundles ("routine kits"), limited-time discounts,
  gifts with purchase, money-back guarantee, first-order discount, referral codes.
- Prefer value-adds (bundles, gifts) over deep discounts when margins are thin.

## 8. Competitor research

- Ads running **30+ days** in the Ad Library are probably profitable — study their hook, angle, offer and format.
- Track competitor offers and prices weekly; react to changes with your own angle, not by copying.
- Competitor sales are not public: rankings here are **estimates** from observable signals.
